# TWEET-TO-YOURSELF Field Guide

This document deliberately separates **fictional conspiracy satire** from
ordinary, testable science. The mock "AI" does not receive signals, diagnose
surveillance, monitor users or communicate with any agency. All names in the
alerts are used in exaggerated fictional jokes, not factual accusations.

## Protocol 005: grass-based counterintelligence

**FAKE WARNING:** "Mossad is listening to your farts. Go outside, relieve
yourself, then touch grass five times to counter their signals."

**REALITY:** Touching grass neither prevents surveillance nor interferes with
radio communications. The punchline is to log off and enjoy being outdoors.
The web app's five touches are simply a counter.

## How to make a tinfoil hat (for comedic purposes only)

1. Find a small, clean piece of kitchen foil.
2. Fold it into a dramatic little crown.
3. Add a paper-straw antenna if you want the full costume.
4. Wear it for the joke or a photograph.
5. Recycle it afterward.

It is *not* an effective protection against intelligence gathering, radio
frequencies or hypothetical mind-reading. Do not attach it to electrical
equipment or claim it offers protective effects.

## Why Earth isn't flat — actual observations

1. **Different shadow angles.** At a single moment, vertical objects at
   different latitudes can cast different shadow angles. The classical
   Eratosthenes construction combines that angular difference with
   distance to estimate Earth's circumference.
2. **Lunar eclipses.** The curved boundary of Earth's shadow seen on the Moon
   is consistent with a sphere at a wide range of orientations.
3. **The sea horizon.** Ships moving farther away tend to disappear hull-first;
   the visibility boundary also changes with observer height.
4. **Different stars at different latitudes.** The altitude of a celestial
   pole changes with observer latitude, and some constellations are visible
   from one hemisphere but not the other.

The Earth is more precisely an **oblate spheroid**, not a perfect sphere.
These observations are part of a much larger body of mutually consistent
geodetic, astronomical and navigational measurements.

## Offline parody design

The fictional threat messages are deterministic entries in
`src/core.mjs`. There is no large language model, browser microphone,
geolocation, device identification, API key, telemetry, tracking request or
network upload. A CSP forbids outbound connections.

Nothing produced by the app should be interpreted as an actual security
warning. Exported receipts are personal joke text saved by the browser
to a file on the user's device.
